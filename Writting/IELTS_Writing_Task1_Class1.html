What is IELTS Writing Task 1?

IELTS Writing Task 1 is the first question in the IELTS Academic Writing exam. The examiner gives you a picture — a chart, graph, table, or diagram — and you must describe what you see in your own words.

বাংলায় সহজ কথায়: পরীক্ষক আপনাকে একটি ছবি দেবে (চার্ট/গ্রাফ/ডায়াগ্রাম), আপনাকে সেই ছবির তথ্য English-এ লিখে বর্ণনা করতে হবে। আপনার নিজের মত বা opinion দেওয়ার দরকার নেই।
Quick Facts — Task 1 at a Glance
What?Answer
Minimum words150 words (লিখতেই হবে — কম লিখলে marks কাটা যাবে)
Recommended words160–180 words
Time20 minutes (Task 2 তে বেশি সময় দিন — সেটা বেশি marks দেয়)
Your jobDescribe the key information — do NOT give your opinion
Marks worth33% of your total Writing band score
Remember: What To Do vs What NOT To Do
✔ DO THIS
  • Describe numbers and trends
  • Compare the most important data
  • Write an overview (main points)
  • Use proper vocabulary
✘ DON'T DO THIS
  • Give your personal opinion
  • Write a conclusion with suggestions
  • Copy the question word for word
  • Write less than 150 words

All Types of Writing Task 1

There are 6 main types of Task 1. You can get any one of these in the exam. Let's learn each type:

📊

Bar Chart (Bar Graph)

Shows data using vertical or horizontal bars. You compare amounts between different groups or time periods.

"The bar chart below shows the number of students who studied science, arts, and commerce in five countries in 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant."
📈

Line Graph

Shows changes over time using connected lines. You describe trends — how data goes up, down, or stays the same.

"The line graph below shows the average monthly temperatures in London and Sydney between January and December. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features."
🥧

Pie Chart

A circle divided into slices. Each slice shows a percentage. You describe the biggest and smallest parts.

"The pie charts below show how people in the UK spent their free time in 1980 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant."
📋

Table

Shows data in rows and columns. You pick the most important numbers and compare them.

"The table below shows the average cost of living in four cities — Dhaka, London, New York, and Tokyo — in 2023. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant."
⚙️

Process Diagram

Shows how something works or how something is made — step by step. You describe each stage in order.

"The diagram below shows how chocolate is produced, from the cocoa plant to the finished product. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features."
🗺️

Map

Shows a place at two different times (before and after). You describe what changed — new buildings, roads, etc.

"The maps below show a small town in 1990 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant."
Tip for Beginners
Bar charts, line graphs, and pie charts are the most common types in the exam. Practice these first. Process diagrams and maps are less common but you must know them. (পরীক্ষায় সবচেয়ে বেশি আসে Bar Chart, Line Graph, এবং Pie Chart — এগুলো আগে ভালো করে শিখুন।)

Marking Criteria — How You Are Marked

Your Task 1 answer is marked using 4 criteria (বিচারের মানদণ্ড). Each one is worth 25% of your Task 1 mark. Let's understand all four:

Task Achievement (TA)

25% of mark
What the examiner checks
Did you answer the question properly? Did you describe the main features? Did you write at least 150 words? Did you write an overview?
Simple tip for beginners
Always write an overview paragraph — 2 sentences about the biggest/most important things you see. Never skip this!
Example
Overview: "Overall, it is clear that sales increased significantly over the period, with the highest figures recorded in 2022."
পরীক্ষক দেখবেন আপনি প্রশ্নের সঠিক উত্তর দিয়েছেন কিনা, সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ তথ্যগুলো বলেছেন কিনা, এবং Overview দিয়েছেন কিনা।

Coherence & Cohesion (CC)

25% of mark
What the examiner checks
Is your writing easy to follow? Do your ideas connect logically? Do you use linking words like however, furthermore, in contrast?
Simple tip for beginners
Use 4 paragraphs always: Introduction → Overview → Body 1 → Body 2. Use linking words at the start of each sentence.
Example
"Sales rose sharply in 2020. However, they fell again in 2021. By contrast, production remained steady."
Coherence মানে আপনার লেখা কি সহজে পড়া যাচ্ছে? Cohesion মানে আপনি কি linking words ব্যবহার করছেন? (However, Furthermore, In contrast, Moreover, etc.)

Lexical Resource (LR)

25% of mark
What the examiner checks
Do you use a variety of vocabulary (শব্দভাণ্ডার)? Do you avoid repeating the same words again and again? Do you spell words correctly?
Simple tip for beginners
Do NOT use the word "shows" five times. Use synonyms: illustrates, presents, displays, reveals, demonstrates.
Example
✘ "The graph shows... The graph shows... The graph shows..."
✔ "The graph illustrates... It reveals... The data demonstrates..."
একই শব্দ বারবার ব্যবহার করলে marks কম পাবেন। বিভিন্ন synonyms শিখুন এবং ব্যবহার করুন।

Grammatical Range & Accuracy (GRA)

25% of mark
What the examiner checks
Do you use different types of sentences? Do you make grammar mistakes? Do you use correct verb tenses (past/present)?
Simple tip for beginners
Use past tense for old data, present tense for processes and maps. Try to mix simple and complex sentences.
Example
Simple: "Sales increased in 2020."
Complex: "Sales increased dramatically in 2020, reaching a peak of 500 units."
Grammar ঠিকঠাক রাখুন। Data যদি পুরনো হয় (e.g. 2010–2020) তাহলে Past Tense ব্যবহার করুন। Process/Map-এ Present Tense ব্যবহার করুন।

Basic Must-Know Vocabulary

These are the most important words for Task 1. Learn all of them. Practice writing sentences with each word.

A. Synonyms of "shows" — (একই অর্থের বিভিন্ন শব্দ)

Never say "shows" every time! Use these words instead:

WordExample Sentence
illustratesThe bar chart illustrates the number of cars sold in five countries in 2022.
presentsThe pie chart presents the distribution of household spending in the UK.
displaysThe table displays the population figures of six major cities.
revealsThe line graph reveals a steady increase in global temperatures over 50 years.
demonstratesThe data demonstrates that more people are choosing online shopping.
depictsThe diagram depicts the stages of water purification.
comparesThe chart compares the income levels of men and women in three sectors.

B. Words for Increase — (বাড়া/উপরে যাওয়া)

Word / PhraseTypeExample Sentence

E. Speed & Size Adverbs — (কত দ্রুত বা কতটুকু পরিবর্তন হয়েছে)

These words make your description more precise and improve your Lexical Resource score.

The 4-Paragraph Structure

Every Task 1 answer should have exactly 4 paragraphs. This is the most important structure you must remember:

ParagraphNameWhat to writeHow long?
1stIntroductionParaphrase (rewrite in your own words) the question. Say what the chart shows, where, and when.2 sentences
2ndOverviewWrite the 2–3 most important features. Do NOT include numbers here. This is like a summary.2 sentences
3rdBody Paragraph 1Describe the first group of data in detail. Include specific numbers, percentages, and dates.3–4 sentences
4thBody Paragraph 2Describe the second group of data. Compare where possible. Include specific numbers.3–4 sentences
সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ কথা: Overview paragraph কখনো বাদ দেবেন না! পরীক্ষকরা সবার আগে Overview খোঁজেন। Overview না থাকলে Task Achievement-এ Band 5-এর বেশি পাওয়া সম্ভব না।

Full Sample Answer — Bar Chart (Band 6–7)

The Question
The bar chart below shows the number of international tourists (in millions) who visited three countries — France, Spain, and the USA — in 2000, 2010, and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Data: France: 70M / 77M / 90M | Spain: 45M / 52M / 83M | USA: 55M / 60M / 79M
Sample Answer — Band 6–7 ≈ 170 words
Paragraph 1 — Introduction
The bar chart illustrates the number of international tourists, measured in millions, who visited France, Spain, and the United States in three different years: 2000, 2010, and 2020.
Paragraph 2 — Overview (Most Important!)
Overall, all three countries experienced a significant increase in tourist numbers over the twenty-year period. France remained the most popular destination throughout, while Spain showed the most dramatic growth.
Paragraph 3 — Body 1 (France & Spain)
France consistently attracted the highest number of visitors. In 2000, it received 70 million tourists, and this figure rose steadily to 77 million in 2010, before climbing further to 90 million by 2020. Spain, by contrast, started with a lower figure of 45 million in 2000 but grew considerably, reaching 83 million in 2020 — an increase of 38 million over the period.
Paragraph 4 — Body 2 (USA)
Regarding the United States, tourist arrivals stood at 55 million in 2000 and increased moderately to 60 million in 2010. By 2020, the figure had risen to 79 million. Although the USA showed steady growth, its increase was less dramatic than that of Spain over the same period.
Note the highlighted words above
The yellow highlights show where good vocabulary has been used. Notice: different synonyms for "increase", linking words (by contrast, regarding, although), and time references (in 2000, by 2020).

Introduction Writing Formula

The introduction is easy if you follow this formula. You just need to paraphrase the question — use different words to say the same thing.

Paraphrase মানে কী? প্রশ্নের সেই একই কথা অন্য শব্দে বলা। আপনি প্রশ্নের শব্দগুলো হুবহু কপি করতে পারবেন না — পরীক্ষক marks দেবেন না।
[Type of chart] + [synonym of "shows"] + [what it shows] + [place/country, if any] + [time period]

Practice Examples — Step by Step

Practice 1 — Bar Chart

Original Question: "The bar chart below shows the number of books sold in three countries — UK, France, and Germany — between 2000 and 2020."

1
Change "bar chart" → The bar chart (keep this, it's correct)
2
Change "shows" → illustrates / presents / displays
3
Change "number of books sold" → the volume of book sales / the quantity of books purchased
4
Change "between 2000 and 2020" → over a twenty-year period / from 2000 to 2020
✔ Final Introduction: "The bar chart illustrates the volume of book sales in the United Kingdom, France, and Germany over a twenty-year period from 2000 to 2020."
Practice 2 — Line Graph

Original Question: "The line graph below shows changes in the average temperature in London between January and December."

1
Change "line graph" → The line graph (keep)
2
Change "shows changes in" → depicts fluctuations in / illustrates variations in
3
Change "average temperature" → mean monthly temperatures / typical temperature levels
4
Change "between January and December" → throughout the year / across all twelve months
✔ Final Introduction: "The line graph depicts fluctuations in the mean monthly temperatures recorded in London throughout the course of a year."
Practice 3 — Pie Chart

Original Question: "The pie charts below show how people in Bangladesh spent their income in 1990 and 2020."

1
Change "pie charts" → The two pie charts / The pair of pie charts
2
Change "show" → present / compare / reveal
3
Change "how people spent their income" → the distribution of household expenditure / the way income was allocated
4
Add: "in two separate years" for "1990 and 2020"
✔ Final Introduction: "The two pie charts compare the distribution of household expenditure among people in Bangladesh in two separate years, 1990 and 2020."

Common Mistakes — Do NOT Do These

These are the most common mistakes beginners make. Read each one carefully.

✘
Copying the question word for word in the Introduction.
✘ "The bar chart below shows the number of students who studied…"
✔ Fix: Paraphrase it — "The bar chart illustrates the quantity of students who pursued…"
✘
Not writing an Overview paragraph.
✘ Going directly from Introduction to Body 1 — no Overview.
✔ Fix: Always write "Overall, …" as your second paragraph. This is MANDATORY.
✘
Writing your personal opinion.
✘ "I think the government should increase funding because the data shows…"
✔ Fix: Just describe the data. Task 1 is NOT an essay. Never write "I think" or "In my opinion."
✘
Writing ALL the data — every single number.
✘ Listing every number from the chart without selecting the important ones.
✔ Fix: Select the MOST important data — highest, lowest, biggest change. Don't copy everything.
✘
Using the same word again and again.
✘ "The graph shows… The graph shows… The graph shows increase… The graph shows…"
✔ Fix: Use synonyms — illustrates, reveals, presents, demonstrates, depicts.
✘
Writing less than 150 words.
✘ Finishing in 100–130 words and moving on.
✔ Fix: Count your words. If under 150, add more detail or expand your sentences. You WILL lose marks.
✘
Wrong tense — using present tense for past data.
✘ "In 2010, sales increase to 500 units."
✔ Fix: Use past tense for past data — "In 2010, sales increased to 500 units."
✘
No numbers in the Body paragraphs.
✘ "Sales increased a lot and then decreased." (vague — no data)
✔ Fix: Always include specific numbers — "Sales increased from 200 to 450 units, representing a rise of 125%."
